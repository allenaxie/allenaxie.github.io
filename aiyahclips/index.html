AiyahClips

Esports reactions from English and Chinese streamers, synced and subtitled.

AiyahClips is a YouTube and TikTok channel. We take the moments that matter in League of Legends esports and show how different streamers react to them, side by side, with English subtitles and translations of Chinese commentary.

Our upload tool

AiyahClips uses a small private tool, run by the channel owner on his own computer, to upload finished videos to the AiyahClips YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API. The tool is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
