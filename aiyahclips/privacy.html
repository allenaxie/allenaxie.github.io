Privacy Policy

AiyahClips upload tool · Last updated November 1, 2026

This policy covers the AiyahClips upload tool: a private program that the AiyahClips channel owner runs on his own computer to upload videos to the AiyahClips YouTube channel. It is not a public app, and no one else signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for permission to upload videos and to manage the AiyahClips channel's videos, for example to change a video from private to public. It uses the official YouTube Data API for this and nothing else.

What we store

The Google sign-in token is saved only on the channel owner's computer. It is not sent to any server we run, and we don't run one. The tool keeps a local log of its own uploads (video titles, IDs and upload status).

What we don't do

We don't collect data about viewers or anyone else, don't sell or share data, and don't use Google user data for advertising. Data from Google APIs is used only to upload and manage AiyahClips's own videos.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time on the Google account permissions page.
